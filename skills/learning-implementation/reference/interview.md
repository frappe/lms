# The interview

Ask in this order. Most people answer all of it in about 30 minutes. Take answers from call notes when you have them, and ask only for what is missing. Write down their words: the plan uses them.

Each answer turns steps in `steps.md` on or off, or fills in a value the step needs.

| # | Ask | What the answer decides |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | What do you want to run: training for your own people, or courses for customers, partners or the public? | **Fit.** Either is fine. Only tracking who attended which HR training: say Frappe HR fits better. Selling courses: payments are not part of this setup, so note it as an ask and start with a free course. |
| 1 | What does "Learning is working" look like for you a month from now? | Their live condition. It must be something you can check on data, not a date. |
| 2 | By when? Is there a date the first group starts? | The pace of the plan, and `batch`. |
| 3 | Who are the learners, and about how many in the first month? | `learners`, and the numbers in `learners-started`. |
| 4 | What is the first course? What material do you have for it today: slides, documents, videos, nothing yet? | `first-course`, `content`. Nothing yet: `content` waits for them, and the plan says so. |
| 5 | Do learners go at their own pace, or as a group with dates? | Group with dates: `batch`. |
| 6 | Do you test what they learned? Can a computer mark it, or does a person need to read it? | Marked automatically: `quizzes`. Read by a person: `assignments`. |
| 7 | Must learners finish each lesson, watch each video or pass each quiz before moving on? | `completion-rules`. |
| 8 | Do learners get a certificate when they finish? | `certificates`. |
| 9 | Who builds courses? Who grades and runs groups? | `course-team`. |
| 10 | Should anyone be able to sign up, or only people you add? What name and logo should learners see? | `basics`. |

## Asking well

- Ask one or two questions at a time, in plain words. Do not show this table.
- When an answer names a number, a name or a list, read it back.
- When they do not know, write "not known yet" and move on. Do not guess for them.
- Start with one course. A plan with five courses before the first learner has finished one is a plan that stalls.
